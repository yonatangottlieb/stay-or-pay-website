Privacy Policy

Welcome to STAY OR PAY (the "App", "we", "us", or "our").

By using the App, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

STAY OR PAY is an app that helps users meet focus and personal commitment goals by creating focus sessions.

Depending on the user's choices, the App may allow the creation of personal commitments that may also involve a financial commitment.

2. What Data We Collect

Depending on your use of the App, we may collect the following types of information:

Account Details

When you sign in with Google Sign-In, we may receive:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (if available)
  • A unique user identifier

We do not receive your Google account password.

Information You Choose to Provide

The App may store:

  • Profile settings

  • Preferred currency

  • Incoming call preferences
  • Contacts connected to your account
  • Usage statistics
  • Session history

Session Information

When you create a session, we may store, among other things:

  • Start time
  • End time
  • Session duration
  • Session status
  • Commitment amount (if applicable)
  • Selected recipient (if applicable)

Technical Information

To operate the service, we may collect:

  • App version
  • Operating system type
  • Internal identifiers required to operate the service
  • Technical diagnostic data in case of a malfunction

Information is collected and used for the purpose of:

Depending on your use of the App, you may be asked to grant system permissions.

Contacts

Used to select people you wish to connect with in the App.

Notifications
